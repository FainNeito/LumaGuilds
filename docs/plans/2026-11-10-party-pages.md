# Party menu and party pages (REQ-205, REQ-206)

Status: approved mockup ("Parties and Diplomacy", 2026-11-10), building on #43.

## Why

Four Parties buttons only said "coming soon": clicking a party, "+N more
parties", Send Party Request and Party Access Settings. Create New Party
already invites guilds and limits ranks, so Send Request and Access Settings
repeat it.

## The menu (3 rows)

- Top row: every active party the guild is in, centred (up to 9). Clicking one
  opens its page. With none, a "No active parties" item sits in the middle.
- Middle row: Create New Party 2, Incoming Requests 4, Outgoing Requests 6,
  How Parties Work 8 (it absorbs the old Party Permissions book).
- Back 22.

Removed: Send Party Request, Party Access Settings, Party Permissions and the
"+N more" item.

## A party's page (4 rows)

- Top: the party card (leader, created, expires, which ranks may join).
- Row 2: the guilds in it, centred; clicking one opens its guild page.
- Row 3: Invite a guild 2 and Leave party 6 for players with Manage Parties;
  Moderate channel 4 for players with Manage Relations. Leave asks for a second
  click. Inviting opens a list of guilds not in the party and sends the
  invitation through #43's consent flow; the guild joins only when it accepts.
- Back 31.
